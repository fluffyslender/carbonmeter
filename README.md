## Three things to know before reading anything else

1. **The Green Claims Directive is not the legal basis for this business.** The Commission announced its intention to withdraw it in June 2025 and the file is not proceeding. The operative instrument is the **ECGT Directive (EU) 2024/825**, applicable since **27 September 2026**, which blacklists offset-based neutrality claims outright.
2. **Target clients (50–200 FTE) are out of CSRD scope permanently.** Omnibus I (in force 18 March 2026) raised the threshold to >1,000 employees and >€450m turnover. Sell commercial pressure — procurement, investors, talent — never legal fear. The **VSME** standard is now the legal *ceiling* on what any customer may ask them for, which makes it the product spec.
3. **Gross emissions and local contributions are structurally severed** — different units, sections, contracts and counterparties. Contribution money never passes through the LDA. Contributions are never expressed in tonnes.

## Locked decisions

- **Snapshot-first go-to-market.** Tier 0 (€2,200) is the entry point, never the €7,500 audit. It is also the highest €/founder-hour product in the portfolio.
- **Analyst hire before software.** The month-9 capacity decision resolves to hiring, not building. Revisit a build at month 18+, once VSME, revised ESRS and GHG Protocol market-based guidance have settled.
- **Contribution model, not credits.** Contribution money never passes through the LDA; contributions are never expressed in tonnes.
- **No commissions from anyone** — project partners, SAF suppliers, venues, offsite organisers. Published policy.
- **Module A repriced €3,000 → €4,000** and restricted to the six-city roster; new-destination diligence quoted separately at cost.
