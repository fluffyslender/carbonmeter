## 1. Definitions

**"Agreement"** means this Master Services Agreement together with all Schedules and every executed Statement of Work.

**"Contribution"** means a voluntary payment by Client to a Local Impact Partner, made directly and not through Supplier.

**"Deliverable"** means any report, statement, table, workbook, dossier, dashboard or other output identified as a deliverable in a SOW.

**"Emission Factor Set"** means the specific published set and version of greenhouse gas emission factors identified in the Methodology Statement for a given engagement.

**"Claim Language Pack"** means the drafting standard for environmental claims issued by Supplier to Client as a Deliverable, as amended from time to time.

**"Client Data"** means all data, records, exports and information provided by Client to Supplier for the performance of the Services.

**"Local Impact Partner"** means a third-party organisation carrying out restoration or environmental work, identified by Supplier to Client under a SOW.

**"Methodology Statement"** means the document at **Schedule 1**, as supplemented per engagement, setting out the calculation methodology, boundary, Emission Factor Set, method hierarchy and coverage basis applicable to the Services.

**"Reported Figure"** means any quantified emissions figure stated in a Deliverable.

**"Services"** means the services described in a SOW.

**"SOW"** means a Statement of Work executed by both Parties under this Agreement.

## 2. Structure and precedence

2.1 This Agreement governs all Services. Each engagement is described in a separate SOW. No SOW takes effect until signed by both Parties.

2.2 Where there is conflict, the order of precedence is: (a) this Agreement; (b) the Data Processing Agreement at **Schedule 2**; (c) the applicable SOW; (d) the Methodology Statement; (e) any other Schedule. A SOW may vary this Agreement only where it expressly identifies the clause varied.

2.3 Client purchase orders, portals and standard terms have no contractual effect, whether or not Supplier references a purchase order number on an invoice.

## 3. Services

3.1 Supplier will perform the Services with the reasonable skill and care of a competent professional consultancy in this field.

3.2 Supplier performs the Services in accordance with the Methodology Statement. The Methodology Statement is contractual. Supplier may update it to reflect revisions to the GHG Protocol, the Emission Factor Set or applicable reporting standards, and will notify Client in writing of any update affecting a Deliverable in progress.

3.3 Supplier may use subcontractors provided Supplier notifies Client and remains responsible for the Services. Subcontractors engaged to process Client Data are subject to Schedule 2.

3.4 Anything not expressly stated as in scope in a SOW is out of scope.

## 4. Client obligations

4.1 Client will: (a) nominate a single named **Data Owner** with authority and system access, stated in the SOW; (b) provide Client Data in the format and by the dates in the SOW; (c) respond to Supplier's written queries within **[[QUERY_DAYS]] business days**; (d) provide accurate and complete information; and (e) nominate an approver for each Deliverable.

4.2 **Client is responsible for the accuracy and completeness of Client Data.** Supplier will apply the validation and reconciliation procedures in the Methodology Statement, but does not independently verify Client Data and is not liable for a Reported Figure that is affected by inaccurate, incomplete or withheld Client Data.

4.3 Client will provide Client Data in pseudonymised form in accordance with **Schedule 3 (Data Handling)**, substituting employee reference numbers for names and home airport codes for residential addresses. Client retains the mapping and will not provide it to Supplier.

4.4 **Delay.** Where Client Data or a required response is outstanding more than **[[DELAY_DAYS]] business days** after the date in the SOW, Supplier may by written notice: (a) re-schedule delivery; (b) suspend the engagement; and/or (c) where the delay exceeds **[[LONG_DELAY_DAYS]] business days**, treat the engagement as complete to the extent performed, deliver work in progress, and invoice the full SOW fee.

## 5. Fees, invoicing and payment

5.1 Fees are stated in the SOW and are exclusive of IVA and other taxes.

5.2 Unless the SOW states otherwise, project fees are invoiced **50% on execution of the SOW and 50% on delivery**. Recurring fees are invoiced monthly in advance.

5.3 Invoices are payable within **[[PAYMENT_DAYS]] days**. Overdue amounts bear interest at the statutory rate applicable to commercial transactions under Portuguese law.

5.4 **IVA.** Where Client is a taxable person established in another EU Member State and provides a valid VAT identification number verified against VIES, the reverse charge applies and Supplier invoices without IVA. Where Client is established in Portugal, IVA is charged at the applicable rate. Where Client is established outside the EU, the supply is outside the scope of Portuguese IVA. Client will promptly notify Supplier of any change to its VAT status or registration.

5.5 Supplier may suspend the Services where an invoice is more than **[[SUSPENSION_DAYS]] days** overdue, on written notice. Suspension does not relieve Client of payment obligations.

5.6 Fees are non-refundable once the corresponding work has been performed. Where a SOW is terminated by Client for convenience, Client pays for work performed to the date of termination plus any committed third-party costs.

## 6. Deliverables and acceptance

6.1 Client has **[[ACCEPTANCE_DAYS]] business days** from delivery to notify Supplier in writing of any failure of a Deliverable to conform to the SOW, identifying the non-conformity. Absent such notice the Deliverable is accepted.

6.2 Where Client validly notifies a non-conformity, Supplier will correct it within a reasonable period at no further charge. **Correction is Client's sole remedy for a non-conforming Deliverable**, subject to clause 15.

6.3 Requests for changes to scope, additional analysis or additional Deliverables are handled by written change order signed by both Parties, with any fee adjustment stated. Supplier is not obliged to perform work outside an executed SOW.

## 7. Restatement, error and withdrawal

7.1 A Reported Figure is prepared on the Emission Factor Set and boundary stated in the Methodology Statement. **Reported Figures prepared on different Emission Factor Sets are not comparable without restatement.** Client acknowledges this and will not publish a comparison between periods unless Supplier has confirmed in writing that the earlier period has been restated on a consistent basis.

7.2 Where Supplier becomes aware of a material error in a Deliverable, Supplier will notify Client promptly, and where the error arose from Supplier's performance, correct it at no charge.

7.3 Where a Deliverable contains a material error, Supplier may by written notice require Client to cease publishing or distributing the affected Reported Figure and any claim derived from it. Client will comply within **[[WITHDRAWAL_DAYS]] business days** and will notify any third party to whom it has provided the affected figure.

7.4 Restatement arising from a change in Client's boundary, a correction to Client Data, or a revision to the Emission Factor Set is chargeable at the rates in the applicable SOW or Supplier's then-current rate card.

### Questions for counsel

1. **Clause 15.1 and the liability cap.** Portuguese law restricts advance limitation of liability, and exclusion for *dolo* and *culpa grave* is generally void. Confirm 15.1 is drawn widely enough to keep 15.3 enforceable, and confirm whether *culpa grave* must be carved out in these terms.

2. **Clause 15.3 — cap construction.** Drafted as the *greater* of fees paid and a floor. Confirm this is the right shape for a business whose engagements are €2,200–€7,500 but whose figures travel to enterprise auditors. A fees-only cap on a €2,200 Snapshot may be commercially indefensible to a client and simultaneously inadequate to us; consider whether the floor should instead be a ceiling.

3. **Clause 14 — third-party reliance.** The single largest exposure in this business model: Deliverables are deliberately designed to be handed to a client's enterprise customer and that customer's auditor. Confirm 14.2 is effective under Portuguese law against a non-contracting third party, and whether any additional step (a legend on the face of each Deliverable, for instance) is needed. **Consider requiring the disclaimer legend on every Deliverable page as a matter of standard practice.**

4. **Clause 12 — non-assurance.** Confirm the wording does not inadvertently describe a regulated activity, and that nothing in our marketing contradicts it. The clause is worthless if the website says "audit" in a way that implies assurance. Review both together.

5. **Clause 16.5 — indemnity.** An indemnity from a 120-person company for a regulatory penalty may be commercially resisted. Confirm fallback position: is a simple allocation of responsibility plus the withdrawal right in 16.4 sufficient protection without the indemnity?

6. **Clause 11.2 — no handling of Contributions.** Confirm this keeps us clear of payment services regulation (PSD2 / DL 91/2018) and that there is no circumstance in the intended operating model where funds could be construed as passing through us.

7. **Clause 5.4 — IVA.** Confirm the reverse-charge and out-of-scope wording, VIES validation obligation, and the *declaração recapitulativa* consequences. Confirm treatment for a non-EU client with an EU establishment.

8. **Clause 3.2** — updating the Methodology Statement unilaterally. Confirm this is enforceable and whether Client consent should be required where an update changes a Reported Figure already delivered.

9. **Annex A of the DPA (Schedule 2)** — confirm the pseudonymisation obligation in 4.3 is drafted such that, if Client fails to pseudonymise, we are not thereby exposed; consider an express right to reject non-conforming data.

### Deliberate drafting choices, for your own reference

- **4.4 (Delay)** is the clause that makes fixed-fee delivery viable. It converts a stalled engagement from an open-ended cost into a closed, invoiced one.
- **7.1 and 7.3** exist because emission factors are revised annually and the business depends on year-on-year comparisons. Without 7.1, a client publishes an invalid comparison and it becomes our problem.
- **11.4 (no commission)** is a warranty rather than a policy statement, deliberately. It is a selling point and costs nothing, because we do not take commissions.
- **12.4 and 16.3** together are the load-bearing pair: we review claims for factual accuracy, and that review must not be construed as legal advice or as assuming responsibility. If those two clauses drift apart in negotiation, the Claim Language Pack becomes a liability rather than an asset.
- **8.1** keeps the workbooks, templates and classification tables ours. That library is the firm's principal asset; do not concede it in a negotiation over a €7,500 engagement.
